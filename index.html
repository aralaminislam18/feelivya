Feelivya is an upcoming social media app designed around the simple idea that online spaces should help people feel connected. Share posts, watch reels, chat with friends and build communities, all in one place.

Feelivya is built by Orame and arrives on the Play Store on 1 December 2026. Early access members get in first.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

When does Feelivya launch?

Feelivya goes public on the Google Play Store on 1 December 2026. Early access members get in first.

How do I get early access?

Enter the Gmail address you use on the Play Store in the Early Access form. We will email you a link to download and use the app.

Who can use Feelivya?

You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country). Read our Child Safety Standards and Terms of Service.

How is my data handled?

We do not sell your personal data. See the Privacy Policy for details and the Data Safety page for a short summary.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time: inside the app under Settings, or by email. Full steps are on the Delete Account page.

How do I send feedback?

Use the feedback form on this page, or email support@feelivya.com. Your name is optional.
